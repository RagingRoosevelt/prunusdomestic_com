From time-to-time I take on commission sewing projects. Send me an email with a description of your project, your target timeline, and any reference photos, and I will put together an estimate. Depending on the size of the project, I typically require payment for materials up front and full payment before delivery. If your project is large enough, we can work out milestones for partial delivery / partial payment.
