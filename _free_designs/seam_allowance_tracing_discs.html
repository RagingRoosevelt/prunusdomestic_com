Use these discs to trace around your pattern to add an offset to your pattern. This allows you to increase an existing seam allowance or add seam allowance where there wasn't any before. They come in metric sizes from 5mm to 25mm in 5mm increments, and imperial sizes from 1/8" to 1" in 1/8" increments. If you need a different size, the CAD is available as a customizable document so you can generate your own from 2mm to 100mm. It's on Printables if you want to make your own.
